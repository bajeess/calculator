Rates, rules and references

Every figure the calculator uses is listed here so you can check it against the law before you file. Bangladesh's income year runs 1 July to 30 June; the assessment year that follows it is the year the return is filed and assessed.

Investment rebate — section 78

  • The rebate is the lowest of 3% of total income, 10% of eligible investment, and Tk 7,50,000.
  • Item ceilings: deposit pension scheme Tk 1,20,000; savings certificates and government securities Tk 5,00,000; life insurance premium up to 10% of the sum assured; listed securities and mutual funds up to the level where the 10% rebate meets its Tk 7.5 lakh ceiling.
  • Exempt income, reduced-rate income, share of profit from a firm or association, and income under final tax are left out of the 3% base.
  • Section 78 does not itself cancel the rebate solely because filing is late; filing-date additional tax is computed separately, and specified exemptions may still be denied under the late-return rules.

How each head is computed

  • Employment — everything in gross, then the lower of one-third or Tk 5,00,000 comes out. Cars are valued at Tk 15,000 to Tk 50,000 a month by engine size; accommodation at annual value or the rent the employer bears.
  • Rent — standard deduction of 25% for residential and 30% for commercial use, plus insurance, loan interest and municipal charges; special rental income is then added before the whole rent-head result is set off against other ordinary income.
  • Agriculture — half of gross receipts is the deemed cost of production where no books are kept. Tk 2,00,000 is non-assessable for a professional farmer with no other income.
  • Capital gains — slab rates within five years; after five years, the higher of 15% and the section 125 tax collected at deed registration. That collection is minimum tax under section 163, so it is set against the charge but is not refundable.
  • Other sources — lottery and game winnings at 25% on the gross receipt, with nothing deductible.

The law behind this, and where to check it

  • Income Tax Act 2023 (Act No. XII of 2023) — the governing statute, which repealed the Income-tax Ordinance 1984.
  • Finance Act 2026 (Act No. 96 of 2026) — published in the Bangladesh Gazette on 30 June 2026 and effective 1 July 2026. It fixes the rate cards for AY 2026-27 through AY 2030-31.
  • National Board of Revenue — publishes the Act, the Finance Act, the annual Paripatra and the return forms.
  • bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd — the consolidated, up-to-date text of the Act.
  • etaxnbr.gov.bd — the e-return portal for online filing and payment.
  • Rates are also fixed by SROs and general orders, which change far more often than the Act itself.

What the Finance Act 2026 changed

  • Threshold Tk 4,00,000 for AY 2026-27 and 2027-28.
  • Investment rebate cut to 10% from 15%, ceiling down to Tk 7.5 lakh from Tk 10 lakh.
  • Minimum tax a flat Tk 5,000 — Tk 1,000 for first-time filers, with no city-corporation split.
  • Dividend final at 15%, with resident withholding unified at 15%. No refund arises.
  • Four items left the final-tax net: savings-certificate profit, acquisition compensation, export cash subsidy and gains on property transfer.
  • Withholding is no longer minimum tax — excess under ss.89-151 is now refundable or adjustable. Sections 138, 139 and 166(2) stay final.
  • Excess turnover tax carries forward against the next year's regular tax.
  • Refunds are automated where income is only salary, financial assets and agriculture: settled in 120 days, paid within 60 days of application.
  • Rental income rebuilt: warehouse rent added, salami moved in, and advances now yield special rental income.
  • Gold, jewellery, gems and club memberships are capital assets, taxed at 5% for non-traders.
  • Filing runs year-round, with a rebate for filing early and additional tax for filing late.
  • Turnover minimum tax runs 0–3% by class: 1% generally, 3% tobacco, 2.5% sweetened drinks, 1.5% mobile and NTTN, 0.2% for a new manufacturer's first three years.
