Taxpayer particulars

Residence follows either test in section 2: 183 days or more in Bangladesh during the income year, or 90 days or more in that year plus 365 days across the four preceding years — so you can be resident despite spending most of the year abroad. This page does not vary the territorial scope of income by residence: a resident is charged on worldwide income, a non-resident on Bangladesh-source or Bangladesh-received income. Enter only the amounts chargeable in your case.
Filing from abroad — the definition of Tax Day in section 2 gives a natural person residing abroad the 90th day from the day of return to Bangladesh, where that person is on leave for higher education, on deputation or lien, or holds a valid visa and permit to earn. The Commissioner may allow a further 90 days on written application made before the due date, and a Tax Day falling on a public holiday moves to the next working day. The fixed windows above model none of these, so the late-filing charge will read high in such a case.

Rates, rules and references

Every figure the calculator uses is listed here so you can check it against the law before you file. Bangladesh's income year runs 1 July to 30 June; the assessment year that follows it is the year the return is filed and assessed.

Checked to 19 August 2026 against the enacted Finance Act 2026. Later SROs, general orders or Paripatra may change the result — check current NBR material before you file.

Investment rebate — section 78

  • The rebate is the lowest of 3% of total income, 10% of eligible investment, and Tk 7,50,000.
  • Item ceilings: deposit pension scheme Tk 1,20,000; savings certificates and government securities Tk 5,00,000; life insurance premium up to 10% of the sum assured; listed securities and mutual funds up to the level where the 10% rebate meets its Tk 7.5 lakh ceiling.
  • Exempt income, reduced-rate income, share of profit from a firm or association, and income under final tax are left out of the 3% base.
  • Section 78 does not itself cancel the rebate solely because filing is late; filing-date additional tax is computed separately, and specified exemptions may still be denied under the late-return rules.

How each head is computed

  • Employment — everything in gross, then the lower of one-third or Tk 5,00,000 comes out. Cars are valued at Tk 15,000 to Tk 50,000 a month by engine size; accommodation at annual value or the rent the employer bears.
  • Rent — standard deduction of 25% for residential and 30% for commercial use, plus insurance, loan interest and municipal charges; special rental income is then added before the whole rent-head result is set off against other ordinary income.
  • Agriculture — under section 43, 60% of the market value of the produce is the allowed expenditure where no books are kept. Under Sixth Schedule Part 1 paragraph (20), Tk 5,00,000 is non-assessable where there is no income under any head other than agriculture and financial assets.
  • Capital gains — slab rates within five years; 15% after five years. The section 125 tax collected at deed registration is credited against that charge and, from AY 2026-27, any excess is refundable.
  • Other sources — lottery and game winnings at 25% on the gross receipt, with nothing deductible.

The law behind this, and where to check it

  • Income Tax Act 2023 (Act No. XII of 2023) — the governing statute, which repealed the Income-tax Ordinance 1984.
  • Finance Act 2026 (Act No. 96 of 2026) — published in the Bangladesh Gazette on 30 June 2026 and effective 1 July 2026. It fixes the rate cards for AY 2026-27 through AY 2030-31.
  • National Board of Revenue — publishes the Act, the Finance Act, the annual Paripatra and the return forms.
  • bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd — the consolidated, up-to-date text of the Act.
  • etaxnbr.gov.bd — the e-return portal for online filing and payment.
  • Rates are also fixed by SROs and general orders, which change far more often than the Act itself.

What the Finance Act 2026 changed

  • Threshold Tk 4,00,000 for AY 2026-27 and 2027-28.
  • Investment rebate cut to 10% from 15%, ceiling down to Tk 7.5 lakh from Tk 10 lakh.
  • Minimum tax a flat Tk 5,000 — Tk 1,000 for first-time filers, with no city-corporation split.
  • Dividend charged at a flat 15%, with resident withholding unified at 15%, so the amount deducted and the tax due normally match exactly. Whether the deduction is a final settlement or merely adjustable is contested: the Finance Act 2026 list of items leaving the final-tax net does not name dividend, but nor does it keep it there. This calculator treats the 15% as a rate rule and the deduction as adjustable, so an excess can come back.
  • Items leaving the final-tax net: savings-certificate profit, acquisition compensation, export cash subsidy and gains on the transfer of property. Withholding on these is now adjustable and refundable; only sections 138 and 139, and collections from persons exempt from filing under section 166(2), remain final.
  • Withholding is no longer minimum tax — excess under ss.89-151 is now refundable or adjustable. Sections 138, 139 and 166(2) stay final.
  • Excess turnover tax carries forward against the next year's regular tax.
  • Refunds are automated where income is only salary, financial assets and agriculture: settled in 120 days, paid within 60 days of application.
  • Rental income rebuilt: warehouse rent added, salami moved in. From AY 2026-27 the replaced section 39 makes an adjustable advance taken outside the banking channel income in full once the aggregate passes Tk 5 lakh, and a non-adjustable advance income in the year received, spreadable over five years at the taxpayer's option.
  • Gold, jewellery, gems and club memberships are capital assets, taxed at 5% for non-traders.
  • Filing runs year-round, with a rebate for filing early and additional tax for filing late.
  • Turnover minimum tax runs 0–3% by class: 1% generally, 3% tobacco, 2.5% sweetened drinks, 1.5% mobile and NTTN, 0.2% for a new manufacturer's first three years.

Read this before you rely on a number. This is a computation aid. It is not legal, tax, financial, investment or accounting advice, and must not be relied on as such. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or hold any savings certificate, DPS, share, mutual fund, insurance policy or other product; the fact that an investment attracts a rebate does not make it suitable for you.

Hard cases turn on facts this page cannot see: your residential status, what a receipt really is, whether an expense is allowed, or how a particular SRO applies to you. These are simplified or left out: [1] partnership share of profit, [2] foreign tax credit, [3] loss set-off and carry-forward, [4] reduced-rate income with the rebate, [5] tobacco: 45% rate, 2.5% surcharge, [6] turnover tax on exempt or reduced-rate receipts, [7] Aadhi, Borga or Bhaga share arrangements, [8] resident and non-resident income scope.

Two assumptions are worth knowing. Where a business pays the turnover minimum, the Act does not say how much regular tax counts as tax on that business, so this page splits it by the business share of total income — a fair reading, not a rule in the statute. And the rate card runs to 2030-31 because the Finance Act 2026 set five years of rates at once, but only the rates; the other rules for those years are assumed unchanged.
